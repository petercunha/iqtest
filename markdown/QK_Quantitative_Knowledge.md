This subtest contains up to 27 multiple-choice questions and assesses quantitative reasoning abilities.
Questions will be timed per item. Work quickly and carefully.
No external aids (calculators, Google, AI, etc.) are allowed. However, a formula sheet will be available during the test.
Pen and paper is strongly recommended for this subtest.
If you do not have a high school level of math fluency, you may skip this subtest.
This test is designed to assess your quantitative reasoning abilities rather than mathematical knowledge. However, questions will require basic mathematical fluency up to the high school level.
