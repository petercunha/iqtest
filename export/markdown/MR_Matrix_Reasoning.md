# Matrix Reasoning (MR)

- **Domain:** Fluid Reasoning
- **URL:** https://cognitivemetrics.com/test/CORE/MR
- **Time limit:** 120 seconds per question
- **Items:** 29

## Instructions

This subtest contains up to 29 questions and assesses fluid reasoning using novel puzzles.
For your viewing experience, it is highly recommended to take this test on a computer.
Each question will be timed 2 minutes per item. Work carefully.
No external aids (Google, notes, scratch paper, etc.) are allowed.
You may attempt some sample items on the next page before the test begins.
